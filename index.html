PART A: Display Technology Fundamentals
டிஸ்ப்ளே தொழில்நுட்ப அடிப்படைகள்

A1. Display-ல் எத்தனை Layers இருக்கு?

ஒரு smartphone display என்பது ஒரே glass piece இல்லை — அது பல layers-ன் sandwich. LCD மற்றும் OLED-ல் layers வேறுபடும்.

📱 LCD Display — Layer Structure (மேல் இருந்து கீழ்):

Layer # Layer Name (English) விளக்கம் (Tamil) Function
1 Cover Glass (Gorilla Glass) பாதுகாப்பு கண்ணாடி Scratch & impact protection
2 Touch Digitizer (ITO sensor) டச் சென்சார் Finger touch detect செய்யும்
3 OCA (Optically Clear Adhesive) ஒளியியல் ஒட்டும் படலம் Glass-ஐ display-ல் ஒட்டும்
4 Front Polarizer முன் துருவமாக்கி Light filter — contrast improve
5 Color Filter நிற வடிகட்டி RGB colors create
6 Liquid Crystal Layer திரவ படிக அடுக்கு Light-ஐ block/pass செய்யும்
7 TFT Layer (Thin-Film Transistor) மெல்லிய திரைப்படத் திசைமாற்றி ஒவ்வொரு pixel-ஐயும் control
8 Rear Polarizer பின் துருவமாக்கி Backlight-ஐ filter
9 Light Guide Plate ஒளி வழிகாட்டி Backlight-ஐ evenly distribute
10 Backlight (LED array) பின்னொளி White light source — LCD-க்கு அவசியம்
LCD-ல் total: ~10 layers

📱 OLED/AMOLED Display — Layer Structure (மேல் இருந்து கீழ்):

Layer # Layer Name (English) விளக்கம் (Tamil) Function
1 Cover Glass (Gorilla Glass) பாதுகாப்பு கண்ணாடி Scratch & impact protection
2 Touch Sensor (On-cell/In-cell) டச் சென்சார் Touch detection — display-ல் integrated
3 OCA/Adhesive ஒளியியல் ஒட்டும் படலம் Bonding layer
4 Circular Polarizer வட்ட துருவமாக்கி Internal reflections prevent — true blacks
5 Encapsulation (TFE) அடைப்பு அடுக்கு Moisture/oxygen-ல் இருந்து organic materials-ஐ காக்கும்
6 Cathode (Metal electrode) எதிர்மின்வாய் Electrons inject
7 Electron Transport Layer (ETL) எலக்ட்ரான் கடத்தி Electrons-ஐ emissive layer-க்கு கடத்தும்
8 Emissive Layer (EML) ❤️ ஒளி உமிழும் அடுக்கு இங்கேதான் LIGHT உருவாகிறது! RGB organic compounds
9 Hole Transport Layer (HTL) நேர்மின் கடத்தி Positive charges-ஐ கடத்தும்
10 Anode (ITO - transparent) நேர்மின்வாய் Holes inject — transparent
11 TFT Backplane (LTPS/LTPO) திசைமாற்றி தளம் ஒவ்வொரு pixel-க்கும் current control
12 Substrate (Polyimide/Glass) அடித்தளம் Foundation — flexible phones-ல் polyimide
OLED-ல் total: ~12 layers
முக்கிய வித்தியாசம்: OLED-ல் Backlight இல்லை! ஒவ்வொரு pixel-ம் தானே ஒளி உருவாக்கும் (Self-emissive).

🔬 LCD vs OLED — Side-by-Side Comparison:

Feature LCD (IPS) OLED (AMOLED)
Light Source External backlight (LED) Self-emissive (each pixel)
Total Layers ~10 ~12
Backlight ✅ Yes — always ON ❌ No backlight needed
True Black ❌ No — grey-ish (backlight bleeds) ✅ Yes — pixel turns OFF completely
Thickness Thicker Thinner
Power More (backlight always on) Less (black pixels = no power)
Burn-in Risk ❌ None ⚠️ Yes — static images degrade pixels
Colors Accurate, natural Vivid, high contrast
Viewing Angle Good (IPS) Excellent
Flexibility Rigid only Can be flexible/foldable
Cost Lower Higher
Repairability Glass-only replacement easier Glass-only replacement harder

A2. Touch Digitizer — எப்படி work ஆகுது?

Capacitive Touch Technology:

உங்க விரல் ஒரு conductor (மின்கடத்தி). Screen மேல் Indium Tin Oxide (ITO) என்ற transparent conductive material coating இருக்கு.

Process:

  1. ITO grid-ல் constant electrical field maintain ஆகுது
  2. விரல் touch செய்யும்போது, அந்த point-ல் capacitance மாறும்
  3. Touch IC (controller chip) இந்த change-ஐ detect செய்யும்
  4. X,Y coordinates-ஐ CPU-க்கு அனுப்பும்
  5. CPU appropriate action எடுக்கும்

Touch Types in Smartphone:

Type Description Usage
On-Cell Touch sensor display panel-ன் மேல் Mid-range phones
In-Cell Touch sensor display panel-ன் உள்ளே integrated Premium phones (thinner)
Out-Cell (OGS) Touch sensor separate glass-ல் Budget / older phones
ℹ️ NOTE: In-Cell technology LCD-ல் touch sensor-ஐ display panel-ன் உள்ளேயே integrate செய்யும். இதனால் display thinner ஆகும், ஆனால் glass-only repair சாத்தியமில்லை — full assembly மாற்ற வேண்டும்.
PART B: Display Defects & Diagnosis
டிஸ்ப்ளே குறைபாடுகள் & கண்டறிதல்

B1. White Dot / Spot — வெள்ளை புள்ளி

🗣️ Customer: "ஸ்கிரீன்ல ஒரு வெள்ளை புள்ளி தெரியுது"

காரணங்கள்:

Cause Type விளக்கம்
Accessibility Feature Software AssistiveTouch / Accessibility Menu enabled — Settings-ல் check
Screen Protector External Air bubble / dust under protector
Pressure Damage Hardware Pocket-ல் அழுத்தம், heavy object வைத்தது — LCD layers shift
Backlight Bleed (LCD only) Hardware Diffuser/reflector layer shifted — light uneven-ஆ leak
Stuck Pixel Hardware Pixel "ON" position-ல் stuck — white/colored dot

கண்டறிதல் (Diagnosis):

Test Method Result Meaning
1 Settings > Accessibility check Software feature = disable செய்யுங்கள்
2 Screen protector remove Protector issue = replace protector
3 Solid white/black/RGB backgrounds display Stuck pixel = color-specific dot; Panel damage = cloudy patch
4 Different viewing angles check Panel damage = angle-independent; Surface issue = angle-dependent

Fix ஆகுமா?

Type Fixable? Method
Software feature (AssistiveTouch) ✅ Yes Settings-ல் disable
Screen protector bubble ✅ Yes Remove/replace protector
Stuck pixel ⚠️ Sometimes Pixel refresh apps (JScreenFix) — 10-60 min color cycling
Pressure/panel damage ❌ No Display replacement only — irreversible
Backlight bleed ❌ No Display replacement only
⚠️ WARNING: வெள்ளை புள்ளியை "massage" செய்து சரி பண்ண முயற்சிக்காதீர்கள்! Internal glass crack ஆகி, damage worse ஆகும்.

B2. Black Dot / Spot — கருப்பு புள்ளி

🗣️ Customer: "ஸ்கிரீன்ல கருப்பு புள்ளி/கறை தெரியுது"

வகைகள் & Fix:

Type Appearance Cause Fixable? Solution
Dead Pixel Tiny single black dot Pixel circuit dead — no power ❌ No (rare fix with pressure) Display replacement
Stuck Pixel (dark) Small colored/dark dot Pixel stuck in OFF state ⚠️ Sometimes Pixel refresh app — color cycling 10-60 min
Ink Stain / Bleed Spreading black patch like ink Physical trauma — drop, pressure, moisture ❌ No — and it SPREADS Urgent display replacement
Debris under glass Small dark speck Dust particle between layers ❌ No (for bonded displays) Display replacement or refurbishment
🚨 CRITICAL WARNING — Ink Stain:
"Ink stain" type black spot பரவும் (spreads)! இது progressive damage. இன்று சிறியதாக இருக்கும், நாளை full screen-ஐ cover செய்யும். Customer-க்கு உடனடி display replacement recommend செய்யுங்கள். Data backup first!

Stuck vs Dead Pixel — எப்படி வேறுபடுத்துவது:

Test Stuck Pixel Dead Pixel
Solid white background Visible as colored dot Visible as black dot
Solid black background Still visible (lit up) Not visible (blends in)
Color cycling test May change color Stays black always
Pixel refresh app May respond No response

B3. Green Line — பச்சை கோடு

🗣️ Customer: "ஸ்கிரீன்ல ஒரு பச்சை கோடு வந்திருச்சு"
📌 IMPORTANT: Green line = Hardware failure. Software update-க்கு பிறகு வந்தாலும், root cause hardware-தான். Update process-ல் ஏற்படும் heat, already-weak connections-ஐ fail செய்கிறது.

காரணங்கள்:

Cause Explanation
Display Driver / Flex Cable Failure OLED screen ultra-thin ribbon cables (ACF bonding) thermal stress-ல் expand/contract ஆகி connection break
Thermal Stress Heavy usage, fast charging, software update → heat → solder joint loosen
Physical Impact Drop, pressure, tight case → microscopic circuit damage
Manufacturing Defect Factory-ல் weak traces / poor bonding → time-over degradation
Moisture Ingress Water damage → corrosion on display flex cable contacts

ஏன் Green Color மட்டும்?

Display-ல் ஒவ்வொரு pixel-லும் Red, Green, Blue (RGB) sub-pixels இருக்கு. Green sub-pixels மற்றவற்றைவிட more sensitive — different voltage requirements. Column/row signal interrupt ஆனால் green pixels "max brightness" state-ல் stuck ஆகும்.

Fix ஆகுமா?

Method Success Rate Notes
Restart ⚠️ Very rare Temporary glitch clear ஆகலாம்
Safe Mode ⚠️ Very rare Software conflict rule out
Diagnostic (*#0*# Samsung) — Hardware confirm — line persists in test
Factory Reset ❌ Almost never Physical damage-க்கு software fix இல்லை
Display Replacement ✅ Only permanent fix Full display assembly change
Laser/ACF Repair (Advanced shops) ⚠️ Specialist only Micro-connections repair without panel change — very rare skill

Customer-க்கு சொல்ல வேண்டியது:

  • "இது hardware problem. Display மாற்ற வேண்டும்"
  • "Phone heavy-ஆ use பண்ணாதீங்க — line multiply ஆகலாம்"
  • "Data backup பண்ணிக்கோங்க — screen fully fail ஆகலாம்"
  • "Warranty check பண்ணுங்க — சில companies free replacement கொடுக்கும்"

B4. Ghost Touch / Auto Touch — தானாகவே டச் ஆகுது

🗣️ Customer: "ஸ்கிரீன் தானா டச் ஆகுது, apps open ஆகுது"

காரணங்கள்:

Cause Type Explanation
Faulty Charger External Non-original charger → EMI interference → ghost touch while charging only
Dirty Screen External Moisture, oil, sweat on screen
Screen Protector External Poor quality protector interfering with capacitive sensor
Grounding Issue Hardware Display frame grounding lost → static charge buildup
Flex Cable Problem Hardware Loose/corroded connector, pinched cable, torn insulation
EMI Interference Hardware Poor shielding → electrical noise → false touch signals
Bent Grounding Pins Hardware Drop → pins bend → puncture flex cable insulating tape
Low-Quality Screen Hardware Third-party replacement screen → poor touch controller
Software Bug Software Rare — OS/touch driver glitch

Quick Diagnosis:

Test Result Meaning
Ghost touch while charging only? Yes Charger fault — use original charger
Remove screen protector → test Fixed Protector issue
Remove phone case → test Fixed Case interfering with touch
Safe mode → test Fixed App conflict
Different charger → test Fixed Charger EMI
Still happens without accessories — Hardware fault — internal inspection needed

Hardware Fix Steps:

  1. Display connector re-seat — disconnect, clean contacts with IPA, reconnect firmly
  2. Check all internal screws — screws complete the ground circuit between display and frame
  3. Inspect flex cable — look for tears, pinches, corrosion
  4. Add Kapton tape — where flex cable touches metal frame (insulation)
  5. Check bent grounding pins — ensure pins don't puncture insulation tape
  6. Test I2C lines (Advanced) — SDA/SCL signal stability between touch IC and CPU
  7. Replace display — if all else fails, the touch controller on the display may be faulty

B5. OLED Burn-in — ஸ்கிரீன் எரிப்பு

🗣️ Customer: "ஸ்கிரீன்ல keyboard/status bar shadow தெரியுது"

Temporary vs Permanent:

Type Duration Fixable? Action
Image Retention (temporary) Fades in minutes/hours ✅ Yes Display varied content, lower brightness
Burn-in (permanent) Never fades ❌ No Display replacement only

Test: Solid gray background display → if shadow visible = burn-in confirmed.

Prevention Tips (Customer-க்கு சொல்லுங்க):

  • Brightness 50%-க்கு கீழ் maintain
  • Dark mode use
  • Screen timeout 30 sec-1 min
  • Gesture navigation use (static buttons avoid)
  • AOD (Always-On Display) moving mode use
  • Static content long time display avoid

B6. Color Tint After Replacement — கலர் மாறுதல்

🗣️ Customer: "Display மாற்றிய பிறகு yellow/blue tint வருது"

Diagnosis & Fix:

Cause Check Fix
Night Mode / Blue Light Filter ON Settings > Display Disable filter
Color Temperature setting Settings > Display > Color Mode Adjust Cool/Warm slider
Accessibility Color Correction Settings > Accessibility Disable
Low-quality replacement screen Compare with similar phone Replace with better quality screen
Flex cable not seated properly Internal inspection Re-seat display connector
Calibration needed Manufacturer tool Use Self Repair Assistant (Samsung) etc.

C2. Step-by-Step Display Replacement

Phase 1: Device Opening (கருவி திறத்தல்)

Step Action ⚠️ Caution
1 Back panel-ன் edges-ல் heat apply (80-100°C) 130°C மேல் போகாதீர்கள் — OLED polarizer damage
2 Suction cup + thin plastic pry tool use Metal tools use செய்யாதீர்கள்
3 Slowly separate — thin plastic card slide around edges Force apply செய்யாதீர்கள் — more heat use
4 Internal screws remove — photo எடுத்து organize Screw locations note — different lengths exist

Phase 2: Internal Disconnection (உள் இணைப்பு துண்டித்தல்)

Step Action ⚠️ Caution
5 Battery connector FIRST disconnect 🚨 இது #1 Rule — Battery first! Short circuit prevent
6 Display flex cable connectors disconnect Plastic spudger only — never metal
7 Other flex cables note & disconnect if needed Photos take for reference
8 Display assembly remove from frame Gentle — heat edges if adhesive tight

Phase 3: Pre-Installation Test (முன் சோதனை) ⭐ CRITICAL

📌 IMPORTANT: இது மிகவும் முக்கியம்! Adhesive apply செய்வதற்கு முன்பே new display-ஐ test செய்யுங்கள்.
Step Action What to Check
9 New display flex cables connect (temporary) —
10 Battery reconnect temporarily —
11 Power ON & test ✅ Display quality — dead pixels, lines, flickering
✅ Touch — all corners, edges, center
✅ Brightness — full range
✅ Color accuracy
✅ Sensors — proximity, fingerprint (if under-display)
12 Power OFF & disconnect Proceed to installation

Phase 4: Installation (நிறுவுதல்)

Step Action ⚠️ Caution
13 Old adhesive 100% remove from frame IPA 90%+ use — every corner clean
14 Frame inspect — bends/warping? Bent frame = display never sits flush
15 Check no internal cables/screws protruding Even tiny protrusion = display gap
16 Apply adhesive (B-7000/T-7000/pre-cut strips) Thin, even bead — excess = problems
17 Align display carefully — seat into frame Press edges gently, evenly
18 Reconnect all flex cables firmly Alignment first, then press
19 Reconnect battery —
20 Clamp or rubber band for curing 30-60 min minimum — 24-48 hrs full cure

Test Item ✅ Check

Touch all areas All corners, edges, center responsive
Brightness full range Min to Max smooth
Auto-brightness Working

PART D: Adhesive Science

D1. Display Adhesive Types — Complete Guide

1. Frame Adhesives (Glass-to-Frame ஒட்டுவது):

Adhesive Color Best For Cure Time Notes
B-7000 Transparent Light/white frames 24-48 hrs full cure Most popular all-rounder
T-7000 Black Black frames/bezels 24-48 hrs full cure Blends with black housing
T-8000 Transparent Precision, delicate work 24-48 hrs full cure Low-odor, professional grade
E-8000 Transparent High-end repairs 24-48 hrs full cure Enhanced formula
Pre-cut adhesive strips — Clean, mess-free bond Instant handling OEM-style — best for consistency
2UUL G301/G302 — Narrow-bezel phones — Cold press — easy future re-repair

❌ NEVER Use:

❌ Adhesive ⚠️ ஏன் கூடாது
Super Glue (cyanoacrylate) Too brittle, damages LCD/plastic, future repair impossible
Feviquick / Instant glue Same problems — permanent bond — no rework possible
Regular double-sided tape Not heat resistant, not optically clear, peels off
Hot glue Too thick, melts internal components, unprofessional
💡 Pro Tip — Color Match Rule: White/light frame → B-7000 (transparent). Black frame → T-7000 (black). Glue residue invisible.
💡 Pro Tip — Thin Bead Rule: Less is more. Too much glue → display sits up (gap) + light leaks from edges + glue seeps into internal components. Thin, consistent line around perimeter only.
💡 Pro Tip — Curing Rule: Set ≠ Cured. Initial set = 3-6 minutes. Full strength = 24-48 hours. Use clamps/rubber bands during curing.

2. OCA vs LOCA — Glass-to-Display Bonding:

Feature OCA (Film) LOCA (Liquid UV Glue)
Form Solid dry film (double-sided) Liquid resin — UV cured
Application Vacuum lamination machine Manual dispensing + UV lamp
Precision Very high — factory grade Lower — messy
Bubble risk Low (vacuum process) Higher (manual process)
Best for Flat screens, mass production, refurbishing Curved screens, irregular surfaces
Equipment needed Lamination machine + bubble remover UV lamp only
Seepage risk None (solid film) HIGH — liquid can flow into backlight/buttons
Rework Easier (heat to soften) Harder (UV-cured permanent)
Yellow over time No Yes — if low-quality or bad UV cure
Industry standard ✅ OEM / Professional ⚠️ Niche / Older technology
📌 IMPORTANT: Professional recommendation: OCA > LOCA for most repairs. LOCA only for special curved screen situations.

D2. Tape Types — When & Where to Use

Complete Tape Guide:

Tape Type Properties Use In Display Repair ⚠️ Warning
Kapton (Polyimide) Heat resistant, electrical insulator, no residue Flex cable protection, heat shielding, insulation between cable & metal frame Gold standard — always keep in stock
Copper Tape Conductive, EMI/RFI shielding Grounding, shielding from interference ⚠️ Conductive! Wrong placement = short circuit
Tesa Tape (Electronics grade) Thin, strong adhesive, clean removal Display-to-frame bonding, component securing Industry standard for OEM-style work
Double-Sided Tape Adhesive both sides Display mounting (if pre-cut strips not available) Use electronics-grade only — correct thickness critical
Black insulating tape Electrical insulation Cable routing, general insulation Leaves residue — not for precision work

Double-Sided Tape — Display பின்னால் ஒட்டலாமா?

Scenario ✅ ஒட்டலாம் ❌ ஒட்டக்கூடாது
Frame edges-ல் ✅ Yes — proper positioning —
Display panel back-ல் full coverage — ❌ No! Pressure on OLED/LCD → damage over time
Thin OLED modules — ❌ No! Excessive pressure → display defects
Original-style die-cut tape ✅ Best option —
Generic craft tape — ❌ Not heat/chemical resistant
🚨 CAUTION: OLED display பின்னால் thick double-sided tape ஒட்டாதீர்கள்! OLED panels ultra-thin, pressure-sensitive. Thick tape → uneven pressure → white spots / dead pixels over time.

Kapton Tape — Advanced Usage:

Usage How Why
Flex cable insulation Wrap around flex where it touches metal frame Prevent short circuit / ghost touch
Heat protection during soldering Cover nearby components with Kapton Components won't get heat damage
Securing cables in safe position Tack cable away from work area Prevent accidental damage
Strain relief Apply at cable bend points Prevent micro-fractures in copper traces
PART E: Advanced Tricks & Pro Techniques
⭐⭐⭐⭐ Expert Level Tricks

E1. Glass-Only Replacement (Refurbishing) — கண்ணாடி மட்டும் மாற்றுதல்

When possible:

  • Display (LCD/OLED) image perfectly working
  • Touch working properly
  • Only outer glass cracked

Professional Process:

Step Equipment Details ⚠️ Notes
1 LCD Separator (Heating Plate) Heat display to soften adhesive (80-100°C) Temperature control critical
2 Molybdenum Wire (0.03-0.05mm) Slide between glass & display panel Wire angle < 30° — prevent polarizer cut
3 Adhesive Remover (X11 liquid) Clean old OCA from display surface Lint-free environment essential
4 Dust Roller / Laminar Flow Hood Remove all dust particles Single dust particle = visible defect
5 New OCA Sheet Apply to new glass or display panel Correct size, no wrinkles
6 OCA Lamination Machine Vacuum + pressure bonding Perfect alignment required
7 Autoclave (Bubble Remover) High pressure removes micro-bubbles Factory-grade finish

E5. Common Mistakes — தவிர்க்க வேண்டிய தவறுகள்

2 Metal tools use for prying Scratched frame, punctured battery Plastic tools only
3 Excessive force when screen stuck Broken frame, snapped flex cables More heat, not more force
4 Flex cable sharp bend (90°) Micro-fractures → display lines/failure Gentle wide curves
5 Skip pre-installation test Bad display installed → full rework needed Always test before glue

8 Wrong screw locations Screw too long → motherboard puncture 🚨 Photo each step, magnetic mat
9 Misaligned connector forced in Bent pins → permanent socket damage Align first, then gentle press
10 Ignoring old adhesive cleanup New display sits uneven → gaps 100% clean before new adhesive
